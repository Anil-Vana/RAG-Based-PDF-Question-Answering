QUESTION 2
which is best among them?

ANSWER
According to the results reported in the PDF, **Random Forest** is the best performing model for the classification task:

- It achieves the highest classification **F1‑score of 0.82** and the highest **ROC‑AUC of 0.89**.  
- These values are higher than those of the Decision Tree, which has an **F1‑score of 0.74** and **ROC‑AUC of 0.81** (see Page 5).

The document does not provide a direct performance comparison between Random Forest and the clustering algorithm (K‑means), because K‑means is evaluated with clustering metrics (silhouette score, Davies–Bouldin index) rather than classification metrics. Therefore, based on the classification metrics presented, Random Forest is the best among the models discussed.  

**Reference:** Page 5 of the PDF.

QUESTION 3
what is the objectiveof this project?

ANSWER
The project’s objective is to **compare several machine‑learning algorithms (K‑Means, Decision Trees, and Random Forests) in order to determine which method performs best for predicting areas with high crime rates (crime hotspots).**  

- This purpose is stated in the project declaration (Page 3).  
- It is reiterated in the system‑requirements discussion, which notes that “the project aims to compare machine learning algorithms … to determine which model performs best in predicting high‑crime regions” (Page 13).
